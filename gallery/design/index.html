Typography is the art of making written language legible and appealing. A good page has a measure of about sixty-five characters, generous leading and margins that are wide enough to hold the page in a hand, and the text is justified with hyphenation so that the gaps between words stay even.

Helium sets this paragraph with automatic hyphenation and justification, the way it sets books and letters. The hyphenation dictionaries are built in and cover many languages.

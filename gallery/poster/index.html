144 writing systems · c. 3400 BC to today

Every way we have written

Writing was invented independently in Mesopotamia, China and Mesoamerica, and perhaps in Egypt. Since then it has taken more than a hundred forms: pictures, wedges, syllables, consonants, alphabets, and scripts that one person designed. Each tile shows the first characters of a script, in order, as Unicode encodes them.

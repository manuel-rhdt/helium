Flags and keycaps

A flag is two regional indicator letters, a keycap is a character with U+FE0F and U+20E3.

Text or emoji?

Unicode says which presentation a character gets: the U+FE0F selector asks for the emoji form, U+FE0E or nothing for the text form of the same character.

In context

Emoji sit in the line like any other character: they take part in line breaking, justification and hyphenation. A sentence can end 🎉 or carry one in the middle, 🌱 grow where it is, and wrap onto the next line without any special handling. The same holds for headings, links and table cells.

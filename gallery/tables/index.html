S-21
Long Pier

Inspection log. The survey team returned to the pier after a spring tide to check the gauge and its reference marks.

Access. The shore approach was open at the start of the inspection. The lower ramp stayed slippery even after the water cleared, so observations were made from the upper landing.

Reference mark. The brass plate on the landward post was visible and firmly fixed. Its stamped reference line matched the mark on the copy of the site sketch.

Gauge face. A band of salt crystals covered part of the lower scale. The team cleaned the surface with fresh water and a soft cloth, then photographed the exposed numbers.

Mounting. No movement was detected at the top fixing. The lower bracket showed a narrow gap on its seaward side. The gap was measured at both ends before the team marked its limits.

Still-water check. Three readings were taken with the gauge in sight from the landing. The water surface rose gently through the series, with only a small scatter from the remaining swell.

Independent check. A staff reading was taken from the landward side of the pier. The staff was steadied against the handrail, and the observation was recorded against the brass reference mark.

Waterline. A thin line of wrack on the landward post marked the previous high water. Its height was logged separately from the gauge readings and included in the site sketch.

Debris. Loose rope was removed from the ladder in front of the gauge. The inspection did not disturb material lodged beyond the end of the lower landing.

Photographs. The team took a wide view of the pier, a straight-on view of the gauge and a close view of the lower bracket. The reference plate is in the gauge photograph for scale.

Second visit. The lower bracket and the landward reference plate should be checked after the next high tide. Readings can continue from the upper landing until then.

Closeout. The last reading was entered in the station book before departure. The team left the access gate latched and returned the staff to the survey store.

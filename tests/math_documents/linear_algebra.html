Spectral Notes on Symmetric Matrices

1. Eigenvalues

Let A be a real 2×2 symmetric matrix

A= ( ab bc )

Its eigenvalues λ1 and λ2 are the roots of the characteristic polynomial det(A−λI)=0, that is

λ1,2 = a+c±(a−c)2+4b2 2

Both are real, and they coincide exactly when b=0 and a=c.

2. Spectral decomposition

By the spectral theorem there is an orthogonal matrix Q such that

A=QΛQT = ∑i=1n λiqiqiT

where Λ=diag(λ1,…,λn) and qi are the columns of Q.

3. The Rayleigh quotient

4. Matrix exponential

Using the decomposition above, the matrix exponential is computed entrywise on the spectrum: eA=Qdiag(eλ1,…,eλn)QT. In particular det(eA)=etr(A).

CHAPTER 1

The Road to Harrowmere

It was the last week of October when Tobias Wren first saw the village, a scatter of grey roofs gathered in the crook of the valley like sheep sheltering from the wind.

He had walked since dawn. The coach had left him at the crossroads with a nod and a bill he could barely afford, and the remaining miles had been rain, mud, and the occasional startled rook.

CHAPTER 2

The Schoolhouse

The schoolhouse stood at the top of Church Lane, its windows dark and its door swollen shut with damp. Tobias put his shoulder to it twice before it gave way.

Inside, the air smelled of chalk and old paper. Rows of desks faced a blackboard on which someone had written, in a careful hand, a single line of arithmetic that did not add up.

CHAPTER 3

What the Ledgers Said

The ledgers went back sixty years. Every entry recorded a child's name, a date of arrival, and a date of leaving, and in the final column a word that changed from decade to decade: apprenticed, removed, called home.

Only one name had no final entry. It appeared on the last page, in fresh ink, above the date of his own arrival: Tobias Wren.
